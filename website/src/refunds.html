01 · What is covered

Monthly and annual subscriptions both qualify. Contact us within 14 days after the initial payment or any renewal payment to request a full refund of that payment. You do not need to provide a reason, and using some AI points does not by itself disqualify a request within this period.

02 · How to request a refund

Email liugq5713@gmail.com with the email used for your purchase and your order or transaction reference. Do not send your full card number, password or activation code.

03 · Cancellation and access

Cancellation and a refund are different actions. Canceling renewal stops future charges and normally leaves access available until the end of the paid period. If you want a refund of an existing charge, request it explicitly.

When a subscription payment is fully refunded, the paid access and unused allowance associated with that payment end, and future renewals for that subscription are canceled. Free core app features remain available.

04 · Other situations

After 14 days, contact support if a charge is incorrect or the service is not working as described. We will review the issue. Your mandatory rights under applicable consumer law, and any applicable rights under Paddle's refund policy, are not limited by this policy.

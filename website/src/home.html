A failed save keeps your words within reach.

Jotway restores the draft for another try. If you have started a new draft, the failed submission waits separately for you to restore it.

Clear boundaries

Your apps stay in your hands.

macOS permissions control app access. Optional AI and indexing can send text to configured services. Typing and local application matching do not call an intent model. Local operation records may include unsent drafts; closing the panel does not erase archives.

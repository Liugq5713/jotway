What would you like to do?

Simulated typing and confirmation, followed by the panel drifting away. No apps open and no data is sent. Click the panel or press Space to pause or resume; Enter confirms the suggested Action. With reduced motion, a static example is shown.

Fewer switches. More follow-through.

From a thought to its next step.

No need to open an app and find a place to type first.

  1. 01

    Open

    Use your global shortcut, the menu bar, the Dock, or the app itself to open the quick record panel.

  2. 02

    Write

    Type a sentence or a few lines of plain text. Shift + Enter adds a new line. Organize it later.

  3. 03

    Check, then confirm

    Review the suggested Action. Switch with ⌥↑ / ⌥↓ if needed, then press Enter to execute.

↶

A failed Action doesn't take your words with it.

Jotway restores the draft so you can retry or choose another Action. If you've already started a new draft, the failed submission waits separately for you to restore it.

AI, when you want it

A little help.
Your decision.

Saving, Google search, and app launching work without AI. Optional Jev intent recognition can suggest an Action. It never executes one for you.

Notes, Reminders, and Calendar can each use optional DeepSeek processing to tidy text or extract dates. Providers require separate configuration and may charge your account. If processing fails, Jotway uses your original text.

Clear about privacy

Your words go where you choose.

Unsubmitted drafts live in memory during the app session. There's no browsable inbox or content history. Confirming an Action may still retain full-text feedback samples locally.
